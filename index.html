Service Management System for Panabo CJ Glass

The proposed Service Management System for Panabo CJ Glass aims to digitize and modernize its traditional, manual workflows into an efficient, tech-driven operation. By introducing a customer-facing portal for seamless service requests and providing employees with real-time access to inventory, records, and order tracking, the system eliminates operational delays and fosters better workplace communication. To ensure high usability and minimize disruption, a structured staff training agenda will be implemented to help employees smoothly adapt to the new technology. Ultimately, this integration aligns the business with modern industry standards and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) trends, significantly boosting productivity, internal coordination, and overall financial performance.
